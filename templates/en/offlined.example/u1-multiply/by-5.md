To **multiply an even number by 5**, take **half** of it and add a **zero**. So 68 × 5 = 340, because half of 68 is 34.

## Why it works

Multiplying by 5 is the same as multiplying by 10 and dividing by 2, because 5 = 10 ÷ 2. The order does not matter: if you divide by 2 first and then multiply by 10, it is much easier to do in your head.

::: ejemplo Example: 74 × 5
::: pasos
Half of 74 is 37
Add a zero: **370**
:::
:::

## With an odd number

If the number is odd, its half ends in **.5**: half of 27 is 13.5, and times 10 that is **135**. Another way: 27 × 5 = 26 × 5 + 5 = 130 + 5 = 135.

::: intenta
a) Work out 52 × 5.
b) Work out 33 × 5.
???
a) Half of 52 is 26: **260**.
b) Half of 33 is 16.5: **165**.
:::
